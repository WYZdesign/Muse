# Live Privacy Policy truthfulness gaps — 2026-09-22

## Deployed verification

Authenticated Settings → Privacy Policy currently states:

- users may request deletion of their account **“and all associated data”**;
- analytics data is collected **“anonymously.”**

The first statement contradicts the adopted retention position requiring narrow legal, fraud-prevention, safety, dispute, and recordkeeping retention. The second is too broad if the service records pseudonymized IP/device/usage data or can link analytics to account/session activity.

## Required remediation

1. Replace “all associated data” with the approved immediate-removal plus narrow-required-retention language across the policy, Help, confirmation modal, support/export copy, and API responses.
2. Describe analytics accurately: categories collected, whether identifiers are raw, hashed, truncated, or pseudonymized, purposes, retention, providers, access controls, and opt-out/rights where applicable.
3. Reconcile data-subject rights, age/identity, recordings/transcripts, media moderation, financial/booking records, and third-party processors with actual operations.
4. Obtain privacy counsel review before any public or invite beta that collects non-test personal data.

## Acceptance evidence

1. Data-inventory and retention matrix maps each collected field/log/media category to owner, purpose, storage, retention, deletion/export behavior, and disclosure.
2. Repository/content search confirms no unconditional all-data deletion promise remains.
3. Production privacy surface is counsel-approved, versioned, focus-contained, and linked from every relevant flow.
