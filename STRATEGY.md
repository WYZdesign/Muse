# MUSE — STRATEGY

> Living doc. Founder vision + advisor assessment. Updated 2026-08-18. Read alongside `HANDOVER.md` (technical) — this is the business/positioning layer.

## One-line thesis
**The verified, consented, protected marketplace for creative shoots — backed by real studios.**

Not a dating app. Not a job board. Not strictly LinkedIn. "Safe creative connection" for anyone who wants to be in front of or behind a camera.

## Founder assets (unfair advantages)
- **FD Photo Studio** — anchor partner, physical supply, credibility. Founder worked there 3 years + was manager of the Chicago location before it closed.
- **FD Photo Mixers** — existing offline two-sided community (photographers + models) that already meets IRL. Pre-seeds the marketplace; solves chicken-and-egg.
- **Firsthand domain pain** — sees the scam/flake/safety problems weekly in the modeling/photography scene.

## Market validation
- **Facebook groups** are the current broken market: TFP (trade-for-portfolio), collabs, paid shoots, mixer events — all at massive scale, with zero verification/consent/payment protection. Proven demand + distribution channel.
- **Studio/space partners** (FD + future) convert the riskiest variable (location) into a managed asset.

## Positioning rules
- **Lead with safety + FD authority**, never "network" or "romance."
- **Promise the process, not the outcome.** Copy says "verified, consented, protected" — NOT "safe, guaranteed." Promising outcomes increases liability.
- **Romance may emerge** (it does in every IRL network) but is NEVER pitched. Public frame is strictly professional + safe. (Airbnb hosts/guests date too; Airbnb never advertises it.)
- **Owner must not use the platform to pursue users** — the single worst optics for a safety-first company. Off-platform only.

## Business model — freemium
- **Free to shoot** (TFP + casual + on-location) — maximizes engagement/acquisition.
- **Paywall = visibility boosts, archive search, premium extras** — monetizes power users, not core usage.
- **Paid shoots** monetize directly via platform fee (~10–20%) + escrow.
- **TFP = acquisition funnel** (free, high volume, builds graph + portfolio); **paid + studio bookings = revenue.** Don't charge for TFP.
- Portfolio flywheel: normie finds photographer → first shoot → photos → update profile → more shoots → eventually paid.

## Studio partnership model
- **Muse = marketplace of record.** Studio sets space rate, talent sets rate, Muse takes platform fee, handles escrow/payments/disputes/verification.
- **Studio incentive** = filling idle hours + free client acquisition + verified/insured clients (studios hate no-shows/scammers/damage too) + safety/liability layer + marketplace SEO.
- **Muse incentive** = verified supply + physical check-in point + venue-side analytics/reviews + revenue share.
- **Check-in as trust sensor:** studio staff confirm arrival/start/end → makes "ongoing tracking/transparency" real without a monitoring team.
- **Leverage FD as the case study:** "we filled X hours at FD" = social proof + FOMO for the next studio.

## Safety model — two layers
1. **Studio shoots (managed environment):** staff, cameras, insurance, witnesses — physical safety net.
2. **On-location shoots (anywhere):** digital safety stack = verified identity (Stripe Identity), signed disclosure + consent, app-based check-in (time-boxed), emergency contact + trusted friend + location share, post-shoot two-way review, fast moderation/reporting.

Both must feel "equally safe" — same digital trust layer, studio adds physical oversight on top.

## Competitive landscape
- **Instagram/Behance/Dribbble** — discovery, no transactions/verification/booking.
- **ModelMayhem/PurplePort** — direct but dated, no modern safety/payments.
- **Peerspace** — ADJACENT: rents spaces by the hour, but does NOT verify talent, handle consent, or settle talent payments. Muse's moat = talent + consent + full transaction; Peerspace owns the space side only. Overlap only if Muse expands into pure space rental.
- **Fiverr/Upwork** — general freelance, not photoshoot-specific with safety.
- **Dating apps** — different intent; the "feels like a dating app" trap is the risk, not a competitor.

## Expansion path (sequenced)
1. Modeling/photography vertical (FD authority) — prove trust + transaction loop.
2. Studio/space network (FD first, then others).
3. MUA / stylist / musician / adjacent creative services.
4. "First-shoot normies" — consumer "safe first shoot" market (headshots, couples, creator days).

## Valuation thesis
- Valuation is a **payments/trust story, not a social-graph story.** Take-rate + escrow + verification-as-a-service is what's fundable; $9.99/mo Pro is table stakes.
- Realistic: niche exit $50–150M (Behance-scale) if it owns the creative-booking trust layer. Unicorn only via expanding into broad creator *transactions*.
- Measure **transactions (bookings closed, dollars moved, repeat rate)**, never raw signups.

## Top risks
1. **Liability** — "we've got your back every step" = more exposure. Promise process, get real counsel (ATTORNEY_HANDOFF.md).
2. **Positioning drift into dating** — kills supply (models) + payment/App-Store risk.
3. **On-location safety** — harder than studio; depends entirely on the digital stack working.
4. **NSFW/adult adjacency** — Stripe/App-Store scrutiny; one minors incident is existential.
