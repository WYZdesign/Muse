## Meaning

This is a pre-cohort environment, not evidence of product-market behavior.
Do not open a 100-person LA cohort until the security, environment, and
operational gates are closed and the first invite cohort has an owner for
support and moderation.

## Measurement cadence

At 10, 25, 50, and 100 invited/activated LA members, record only aggregate:

- invited, activated, profile-complete, and retained members;
- reciprocal conversations and time to first reply;
- useful matches/collaborations, brief applications, and bookings;
- reports, blocks, moderation response time, and unresolved safety queue;
- support volume and critical defects;
- performance/error evidence from the approved monitoring surfaces.

Advance to Chicago only after the LA 100-person gate has measured safety,
support, retention, and density for a defined observation window. Repeat the
same gate before New York.
