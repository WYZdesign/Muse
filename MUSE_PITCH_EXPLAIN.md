# MUSE — HOW TO EXPLAIN IT TO PEOPLE

> Every framing, from a one-liner to a full investor-level pitch. Marked for audience: **casual
> (creator), professional (creative), and investor/partner.** Pick the section that fits the room.

---

## 1. One-liners (as long as needed)

- **6 words:** *Verified creatives. Safe shoots. Escrowed pay.*
- **1 sentence:** "Muse is where people who work in front of — or behind — a camera find each other,
  agree on terms, get paid safely, and get reviewed, so every shoot is protected from first match to
  final review."
- **The anti-pitch:** "It's not a dating app, it's not a job board. It's the trust and payment layer
  for creative shoots that Instagram, ModelMayhem, and Peerspace are each missing half of."
- **The emotional line:** "Every creative wants to be seen, be made into art, be someone's muse. Muse
  is the safe place that makes that happen."

---

## 2. For a creative (the casual pitch)

> *"You know how you find shoots on Instagram DMs or Facebook groups, and it's 90% ghosting and
> 'trust me bro'? Muse fixes that. You make a profile, you verify you're real, and you get found by
> people who actually want to work — or you find them. If it's a paid shoot, the money goes into
> escrow, so nobody gets stiffed. Before the shoot, you both sign what's allowed and you can share your
> location with someone you trust. After, you both leave a review, so your reputation actually
> compounds. And if it's TFP — free, for the portfolio — it's easy, so you can build your book and
> turn it into paid work."*

**Key things to land:**
- No more DM/group-chat minefield.
- Verified identity, signed consent, escrowed payment, trusted contact.
- Two-way reviews that make your reputation real.
- TFP is free on purpose — that's how you get started.

---

## 3. For a professional/industry buyer (the business pitch)

> *"Muse is a professional marketplace for shoots and collaborations. On the host side, booking
> professionals list a rate and get paid through escrow — held until the shoot completes, so nobody
> eats a loss. We take a 15% blended fee, itemized so the booker sees exactly what they're paying. On
> the talent side, agencies, casting directors, and brands get pre-verified, pre-qualified creatives
> with real review history — we're the anti-Instagram for hiring behind and in front of the camera."*

**Key things to land:**
- Verified identity before any paid interaction.
- Escrow: nobody can ghost or get stiffed, on either side.
- 15% blended take (7% host + 8% buyer fee) — the model, not charity.
- Industry tier (Muse Studio) for heavy buyers.
- Trust/reputation is the moat, not the volume.

---

## 4. For a partner / studio / space (FD pitch)

> *"You've got empty hours and a real studio. We bring verified, pre-consented clients and talent who
> want to shoot there. You keep your space fee; we handle the talent, the payment escrow, the safety
> check-ins, and the disputes. You get a physical safety net (your staff is there), a check-in point
> that makes the trust real, and zero cost to join. We fill your calendar and give you a piece of the
> transaction."*

**Key things to land:** filled idle hours, free client acquisition, verified/insured clients, less
no-show/damage, a physical check-in point, and the FD case study ("we filled X hours").

---

## 5. For an investor (the statement pitch)

> *"There's a huge, proven market for creative shoots — TFP, collabs, paid gigs — running today in
> Facebook groups and DMs with no verification, no consent, and no payment protection. Nobody owns the
> **trust and transaction layer** for shoots: Instagram does discovery, ModelMayhem is dated, and
> Peerspace owns the space but not the talent, the consent, or the settlement.
>
> Museum [Muse] is that layer. We monetize two ways: a freemium subscription (Muse Pro $9.99/mo, Muse
> Studio $29.99/mo for industry) and a **15% take on every paid booking** via Stripe Connect escrow. We
> seed supply from an existing LA/NYC/Chicago offline community (FD Mixers) and an anchor studio
> partner (FD Photo Studio) — so we launch with supply, not a cold start.
>
> The moat isn't the app; it's the **accumulated trust data** — verified identity, signed consent,
> escrow history, two-way reviews. That's behavioral data only a running marketplace accumulates, and
> it compounds into switching cost. We measure transactions — bookings closed, dollars moved, repeat
> rate — not raw signups.
>
> We're the **anti-AI platform**: real humans, real shoots, real trust — the exact thing that becomes
> scarce and valuable as AI image generation floods the market."*

## 6. The boardroom cheat sheet (say this if you get one question)

| If they ask | Lead with |
|-------------|-----------|
| "What is it?" | Verified, consented, protected marketplace for creative shoots. |
| "Who's it for?" | People in front of and behind the camera, plus industry/studios who buy. |
| "How do users make money?" | Bookings, paid briefs, industry hires, TFP-to-paid flywheel. |
| "How do YOU make money?" | 15% take on bookings + Pro/Studio subscriptions. |
| "How is it safe?" | Stripe identity verification, signed disclosure/consent, escrow, check-ins, two-way reviews. |
| "Why you?" | Existing supply (FD Mixers LA/NYC/CHI) + anchor studio (FD) + firsthand domain pain. |
| "What's the moat?" | The trust data — verification, consent, escrow, reviews — that compounds. |
| "What's the risk?" | The #1 risk is positioning drifting into dating, and the adult-content/liability line. |

---

## 7. What to ALWAYS say vs NEVER say

**Always (safe):**
- "Verified identity" / "signed consent" / "escrowed payment" / "trusted contact" / "two-way reviews."
- "We protect the process" / "the money is held until the shoot wraps."
- "Real people, real shoots, real trust."

**Never (liability / positioning risk):**
- "We guarantee you'll be safe." (Promises outcomes, not process — legal exposure.)
- Anything that frames it as dating.
- "We're a job board" / "We're like Instagram." (Kills the differentiation.)
- "Unlimited NSFW." (Adult-content + minors risk is existential.)

## 8. The single clearest way to explain it in one breath

> **"Muse is a safe marketplace for creative shoots. You find your people, you agree on the terms,
> you pay through escrow so nobody gets stiffed, you shoot safely with the location shared and consent
> signed, and you both leave a review — so your work and your reputation keep growing. It's where
> being seen gets done properly."**
